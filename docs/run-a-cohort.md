# Running a Cohort

## Before day 1
- [ ] Cap the group. 50 is the ceiling for one trainer correcting daily.
- [ ] Publish the rules before anyone joins. Rules after the fact feel arbitrary.
- [ ] Run a pre-course seminar. It halves the questions in week one.
- [ ] Have every unit PDF built and ready. Do not write them live.

## Daily rhythm
| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 09:00 | Lesson: poster + 3-minute voice note |
| 12:00 | Task: conditions + 21:00 deadline |
| 19:00 | Reminder, with the count of submissions so far |
| 21:00 | Close the task, publish 7 corrected samples |

## Sustainability
Listen to a **sample**, not everything. Attendance is recorded by the voice
note arriving, not by you hearing it. Publish 7 corrections a day — the group
learns from those regardless of whose they are.

Alternate: voice-note day, then AI self-correction day. Halves the load,
keeps practice daily.

## Correcting in public
One good thing, one fix. Never more. A learner who gets five corrections in
front of the group does not speak again.

## Ending
- Send the feedback form on **day 28**, not day 30.
- Make the certificate conditional on completing it.
- Personal DM to everyone who finished. One real, specific observation each.
