# Case Study — Cohort 01

## The problem
550 Arabic-speaking professionals who could read and understand English but
froze the moment someone asked them a question. Not a knowledge gap — a
practice gap.

## The hypothesis
Daily speaking with correction beats more content. One voice note a day,
corrected, for 30 days.

## The design
- 4 weeks, 9 units, 30 minutes a day
- A daily lesson (09:00), a task (12:00), a voice note due 21:00
- 7 missed days in a row = removal
- 6 PDFs, 6 AI prompts, 4 live workshops

## Delivery
| | |
|---|---|
| Lessons published | 30 |
| Tasks published | 30 |
| Voice notes received, peak day | 250+ |
| PDFs produced | 6 |

## Results
| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Enrolled | 550 |
| Completed all 30 days | 250 (45%) |
| Used English in a real situation (Kirkpatrick level 3) | 170 (68% of finishers) |

## Evidence
- Before/after recordings, days 1 and 30
- Unprompted LinkedIn posts from four participants

## What failed
- Correcting 250 voice notes a day was not sustainable past week two.

## What changed because of it
- Cohort 02 is capped at 50.
- Alternating voice-note days and self-correction AI days.
